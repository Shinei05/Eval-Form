## 1. Signing In to the Admin Portal

1. Open the **EduRate** application in your web browser.
2. On the main login page, click the **Administrator** role card (identified by a shield/building icon).
3. In the sign-in modal window:
   - Enter your **Admin Email Address**.
   - Enter your **Password**.
4. Click **Sign In**.
5. You will be directed to the **Admin Dashboard**.

> **Note:** Administrators have continuous 24/7 access to the system, allowing you to configure schedules and manage records even when student and teacher evaluation windows are closed.

---

## 2. Admin Dashboard Overview & Real-Time Monitoring

The Admin Dashboard provides a real-time command center for monitoring school-wide evaluation activities.

### Summary Metrics Bar (KPI Cards)
- **Total Faculty Members:** Total number of registered active teachers.
- **Active Evaluation Period:** Shows whether an evaluation period is currently open or closed, and which period is running (e.g., *1st Period*, *2nd Period*).
- **Student Coverage Rate:** The percentage of teachers who have received student evaluations.
- **Teacher Compliance Rate:** The percentage of teachers who have received peer evaluations from colleagues.

### Navigation Sidebar
The left-hand navigation menu is organized into three main groups:
1. **Dashboard:**
   - *Student Evaluations* (Student evaluation results by teacher)
   - *Teacher Evaluations* (Peer evaluation results by teacher)
   - *Evaluate Teachers* (Admin direct evaluation portal)
2. **Management:**
   - *Manage Accounts* (Add, edit, and archive teacher accounts)
   - *Archived Teachers* (Restore deactivated accounts)
   - *Announcements* (Create and broadcast notices)
   - *Schedule* (Configure period dates and times)
   - *Upload CSV* (Bulk import students and class assignments)
3. **Questions:**
   - *Student Questions* (Student evaluation questionnaire editor)
   - *Teacher Questions* (Peer evaluation questionnaire editor)

## 3. Managing Student & Peer Evaluations

### Student Evaluations Tab
This tab displays all faculty members and their student evaluation progress:
- **Teacher Information:** Name, subject department, grade level badge (e.g., *Elementary (Grades 4–6)*, *Junior High (Grades 7–10)*), quarter, and academic year.
- **Completed Submissions:** Number of student evaluations submitted for that teacher.
- **Average Rating:** The teacher's overall average score (out of 5.00).
- **Sentiment Status:** Performance level indicator (*Very Good*, *Good*, *Average*, *Poor*, or *Very Poor*).

#### Search, Grade Level & Sort Filters:
- **Search Bar:** Search teachers by name or subject.
- **Grade Level Filter Dropdown:** Filter teachers by teaching level (*All Grades & Levels*, *Elementary (Grades 4–6)*, *Junior High (Grades 7–10)*, or specific individual grades *Grade 4* through *Grade 10*).
- **Sort Dropdown:** Sort by *Name*, *Subject*, or *Submissions*.

#### Available Actions per Teacher:
- **View Submissions List:** Click the dropdown arrow to expand the list of individual students who evaluated the teacher (shows Student Name, Student ID, Section, Score, Sentiment, and Date).
- **View Individual Form:** Click on any student entry in the list to open the exact printable evaluation form submitted by that student.
- **View Merged Form:** Click the document icon to open the official consolidated average score sheet.
- **View Comments:** Click the **Comments** button in the evaluation modal to open the dedicated comments feed where you can view, search, and filter all written feedback from students and peers.
- **View Performance Graph:** Click the bar chart icon to view a visual breakdown of scores across each category.
- **Generate Summary Report:** Click the summary report icon to view the full performance report with PDF and CSV export options.

### Teacher (Peer) Evaluations Tab
Displays evaluation progress submitted by fellow teachers:
- Shows colleague evaluation counts, overall peer average, grade level badge, and sentiment classification.
- Features search, **Grade Level Filter**, and sort controls to quickly filter peer evaluations by teaching grade.
- Allows viewing individual peer submissions, merged peer averages, performance graphs, dedicated feedback comments, and summary reports.

### Evaluate Teachers Tab
Direct admin evaluation portal:
- Features search bar, **Grade Level Filter**, and sort by status/name/subject.
- Displays each teacher card with their assigned grade levels, subject, and evaluation status.
- Allows starting an evaluation for pending teachers or viewing results for evaluated teachers.

### Teacher Comments & Feedback Page
When you click the **Comments** button in the teacher evaluation details window:
1. You are taken to the **Teacher Comments & Feedback** portal.
2. **Category Tab Navigation:**
   - **All Comments:** Displays all feedback received by the teacher with a total counter.
   - **Student Evaluations:** Filters the list to display only comments submitted by students.
   - **Peer Evaluations:** Filters the list to display only comments submitted by colleague teachers.
3. **Search & Sentiment Filters:**
   - Search box to find specific keywords or phrases in the remarks.
   - Dropdown filter to view feedback by sentiment (*Good / Very Good*, *Average / Neutral*, *Poor / Very Poor*).
   - Sort selector to order comments by newest, oldest, highest score, or lowest score.
   - Toggle checkbox to show only evaluations with written text or all submissions.

## 4. Conducting Direct Administrator Evaluations

Administrators can evaluate faculty members directly:

1. Click **Evaluate Teachers** under the *Dashboard* section in the sidebar.
2. Browse or search for the teacher you wish to evaluate.
3. Click the **Evaluate** button on the teacher's card.
4. Complete the evaluation criteria using the 1–5 rating scale, enter administrative remarks in the feedback box, and click **Submit Evaluation**.

## 5. Managing Faculty Accounts

To create, update, or search teacher records, go to **Manage Accounts** in the sidebar.

### Switching View Modes
- Use the **Card View** / **List View** toggle at the top right to view teachers as rich cards or as a compact data table.

### Adding a New Teacher Account
1. Click the **+ Add Teacher** button at the top.
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name**
   - **Email Address**
   - **Subject Department** (select from the dropdown)
   - **Grading Quarter** and **Academic Year**
   - **Password** and **Confirm Password**
3. Click **Save Teacher**. The new teacher account is immediately ready for use.

### Editing Teacher Details
1. Locate the teacher in the list and click the **Pencil (Edit)** icon.
2. Update the teacher's name, subject, quarter, or year in the edit form.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

### Archiving (Deactivating) a Teacher Account
1. Locate the teacher and click the **Archive (Trash/Box)** icon.
2. Confirm the action in the confirmation modal.
3. The teacher account will be deactivated and moved to the **Archived Teachers** section. Deactivated teachers can no longer log in.

## 6. Archived Accounts & Recovery

If a teacher was archived accidentally or returns to the institution:

1. Click **Archived Teachers** in the navigation sidebar.
2. Browse the list of deactivated accounts.
3. Click the **Restore** button next to the teacher's name.
4. The account will immediately be restored to active status and reappear in the active teacher list.

## 7. Configuring Evaluation Schedules

To set the dates and times when evaluations are open, navigate to **Schedule** in the sidebar.

### Setting Up Period Dates & Times
1. Enter the **School Year** (e.g., `2025-2026`).
2. Select the period you wish to configure (**1st**, **2nd**, **3rd**, or **4th Period**).
3. Specify:
   - **Start Date** and **Start Time** (when evaluations become accessible).
   - **End Date** and **End Time** (when evaluations automatically close).
4. You can use the **Interactive Calendar** on the right side to pick dates visually.
5. Click **Save Schedule**.

### Schedule Overview & Resetting
- **Period Overview:** Displays all 4 grading periods side-by-side with an active indicator badge showing which period is currently live.
- **Reset Period:** If you need to deactivate or clear a specific period, click **Reset Period** and confirm.

## 8. Bulk Importing Accounts via CSV

To enroll students and assign them to teachers in bulk:

1. Click **Upload CSV** in the navigation sidebar.
2. Prepare your CSV file using a spreadsheet editor (such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets).

### Expected CSV Column Structure:
Your CSV file should have the following column order:
1. `student_email` – Student's email address
2. `student_firstname` – Student's first name
3. `student_lastname` – Student's last name
4. `stud_id` – Student ID / Learner Reference Number (LRN)
5. `student_grade` – Grade level (e.g., `4`, `7`, `10`)
6. `student_section` – Section or block name (e.g., `Diamond`, `Emerald`)
7. `student_password` – Initial password (if blank, defaults to Lastname + StudentID)
8. `teacher_email` – Assigned teacher's email address
9. `teacher_firstname` – Assigned teacher's first name
10. `teacher_lastname` – Assigned teacher's last name
11. `teacher_password` – Initial teacher password
12. `subject_name` – Subject name (e.g., `Mathematics`, `English`)
13. `quarter` – Grading quarter (e.g., `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`)
14. `year` – Academic year (e.g., `2025`)

### Uploading the File:
1. Drag and drop your `.csv` file into the upload box, or click **Browse File** to select it.
2. Click **Upload and Process CSV**.
3. The system will create the user accounts, establish student-teacher assignments, and display a success summary.

## 9. Managing Evaluation Questions & Word (.docx) Import

To customize the evaluation criteria, select **Student Questions** or **Teacher Questions** under the *Questions* section in the sidebar.

### Version Management
- **Version Selector:** Switch between different questionnaire versions (e.g., `v2025`, `v2026`).
- **Set as Active Version:** Click this button to make the currently selected version the active questionnaire used across all student and teacher evaluation forms.

### Importing a Questionnaire from Microsoft Word (.docx)
1. Click the **Import Word Document (.docx)** button at the top.
2. In the popup window, enter a **Version Identifier** (e.g., `v2026-Q1`).
3. Select your `.docx` file containing the official evaluation rubric.
4. Click **Upload and Parse**.
5. The system automatically reads the Word document, identifies category headers and indicators, and saves them as a new questionnaire version.

### Manual Rubric Editing
- **Add New Category Header:** Click **+ Add Category**, enter the title (e.g., *Classroom Management / Pamamahala ng Silid-Aralan*), and save.
- **Edit Header:** Click the pencil icon on any category banner to rename it.
- **Delete Header:** Click the trash icon to remove a category and its questions.
- **Add Question:** Click **+ Add Question** under any category, type the indicator text, and click **Save**.
- **Edit / Delete Question:** Hover over any question to edit its text inline or delete it.

## 10. Publishing School Announcements

To communicate important dates and updates to students and teachers:

1. Click **Announcements** in the sidebar.
2. To create a new announcement:
   - Enter a **Title** (e.g., *1st Period Evaluation Deadline*).
   - Select a **Category** (*General*, *Evaluation*, *Event*, *Urgent*, *Academic*, or *System*).
   - Enter the **Announcement Content**.
   - Check **Pin to Top** if this notice should remain pinned at the very top of all user feeds.
3. Click **Post Announcement**.

### Managing Existing Announcements
- Use the three-dots menu on any announcement card to **Edit** its content or **Delete** the announcement.

## 11. Generating Reports, Printable Forms & PDF/CSV Exports

EduRate provides extensive reporting tools adhering to standard educational formats (including official DepEd headers):

### 1. Printable Individual & Merged Forms
- Accessible by clicking the document icons on the dashboard.
- Displays official logos (**DepEd**, **Bagong Pilipinas**, **School Logo**).
- Displays evaluator info, teacher info, filled score sheets per indicator, total computed average, and qualitative remarks.
- You can print directly to your physical printer or save as PDF using your browser's Print dialog (**Ctrl + P** or **Cmd + P**).

### 2. Visual Performance Graphs
- Accessible via the bar chart icon.
- Renders an interactive bar chart displaying the teacher's average rating in each category section.
- Color-coded performance ranges:
  - **Green:** Excellent (4.50 – 5.00)
  - **Blue:** Good (3.50 – 4.49)
  - **Amber:** Fair (2.50 – 3.49)
  - **Red:** Poor (1.00 – 2.49)

### 3. Comprehensive Teacher Performance Summary Report & Multi-Page PDF Export
- Accessible by clicking the summary report icon on the dashboard or clicking **Export PDF** in the teacher evaluation details window.
- **Side-by-Side Document Views:**
  - **Student Evaluation Document (Left):**
    - **Page 1 (Ratings Breakdown):** School letterhead, teacher profile, overall student rating score and adjectival tier, category-by-category breakdown, signatures, and DepEd footer.
    - **Page 2 (Comments Log):** Click **Page 2: Comments (Next →)** or the next button to view the full chronological log of student feedback notes with submission dates, numerical scores, and sentiment tags.
  - **Peer / Teacher Evaluation Document (Right):**
    - **Page 1 (Ratings Breakdown):** Colleague peer evaluation ratings and category breakdown.
    - **Page 2 (Comments Log):** Click **Page 2: Comments (Next →)** to view all peer feedback and recommendations.
- **Exporting Options (via Save as PDF):**
  - **Student Evaluation:** Downloads a 2-page Legal PDF containing Page 1 (Breakdown) and Page 2 (Student comments log).
  - **Peer / Teacher Evaluation:** Downloads a 2-page Legal PDF containing Page 1 (Breakdown) and Page 2 (Peer comments log).
  - **Export Both Documents:** Downloads a complete 4-page Legal PDF package containing both documents in full.
  - Click **Export CSV** to download raw spreadsheet performance data.

### 4. Consolidated All-Teachers CSV Export
- From the Admin Dashboard, click **Export All Teachers Summary** to download a complete institution-wide CSV file containing all faculty members, total evaluations received, and combined performance averages.

## 12. Administrator Settings & Password Management

1. Click your name or avatar in the top right corner and select **Settings**.
2. **Profile Information Card:** Displays your administrative account email, name, and role.
3. **Change Password Card:**
   - Enter your **Current Password**.
   - Enter your **New Password** (minimum 8 characters with numbers and symbols).
   - Confirm the new password and click **Update Password**.

---

## 13. Signing Out

1. Click your profile avatar in the top right corner (or click **Sign Out** at the bottom of the left sidebar).
2. Confirm sign out. Your administrative session will be terminated safely.
