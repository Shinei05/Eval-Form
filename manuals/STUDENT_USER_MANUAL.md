## 1. Signing In to Your Account

To log in to the evaluation system:

1. Open the **EduRate** application in your web browser.
2. On the welcome screen, look for the **Student** card (featuring a graduate cap icon) and click **Sign in as Student**.
3. In the sign-in window:
   - Enter your **Student ID Number** or your registered **Email Address**.
   - Enter your **Password**.
4. Click the **Sign In** button.
5. If your credentials are correct and the evaluation schedule is currently open, you will be taken directly to your **Student Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Resetting a Forgotten Password

If you forget your password:

1. On the Student Sign-In window, click the **Forgot Password?** link.
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Verification Code**.
3. Check your email inbox for a 6-digit numeric verification code.
4. Enter the 6-digit code into the screen and click **Verify Code**.
5. Type in your **New Password** and confirm it in the **Confirm Password** field.
   - *Requirement:* Your password must be at least 8 characters long and contain both numbers and symbols (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`, `%`, etc.).
6. Click **Change Password**. You can now sign in with your new password.

## 3. Understanding the Evaluation Period

The school administration opens evaluations during designated periods (for example: *1st Period*, *2nd Period*, etc.).

- **When the Evaluation Period is Open:** You can log in, view your assigned teachers, and submit your ratings.
- **When the Evaluation Period is Closed:** If you try to sign in when no evaluation period is active, the system will show the **"Evaluations Closed"** screen. You will need to wait until the administration opens the next scheduled evaluation window.

## 4. Navigating the Student Dashboard

Once logged in, your dashboard is organized to help you see which teachers are ready for evaluation.

### Top Information Banner
- Displays your full name and greeting.
- Displays the active **School Year** and **Evaluation Period** (e.g., *2025-2026 | 1st Period*).

### Password Security Notice
- If your account is using a default password, a yellow alert banner will appear advising you to update your password.
- You can click **Go to Settings** directly from the banner to set a private password.

### Summary Counter Cards
At the top of your dashboard, you will find three quick-glance statistics:
- **Available Evaluations:** Total number of assigned teachers available for you to evaluate.
- **Evaluations Completed:** Number of teachers you have already evaluated this period.
- **Pending Evaluations:** Number of teachers you still need to evaluate.

### Search and Filters
Above the list of teachers, you can use several tools to quickly find a specific teacher:
- **Search Bar:** Type a teacher's name or subject.
- **Subject Filter Dropdown:** Filter the list to display only teachers of a specific subject (such as *Mathematics*, *Science*, *English*, etc.).
- **Evaluation Status Toggle:** Toggle between showing all teachers or hiding those you have already evaluated.
- **Sort By:** Sort teachers alphabetically by name or by subject.

### Teacher Cards
Each teacher assigned to your section is displayed as a card showing:
- Teacher's full name and email.
- Assigned Subject.
- Current grading quarter and academic year.
- **Status Badge:**
  - **Pending / Not Evaluated** (Yellow/Gray): Indicates you still need to submit your evaluation. A blue **Evaluate** button will be visible.
  - **Evaluated** (Green): Indicates you have already submitted your evaluation for this teacher. The button will be disabled to prevent duplicate submissions.

## 5. How to Evaluate a Teacher

1. Find the teacher you wish to evaluate on your dashboard and click the **Evaluate** button.
2. The **Student Evaluation Tool** form will open.

### Form Overview:
- **Header Information:** Displays the teacher’s name, subject, and the current evaluation date.
- **Rating Scale Legend:**
  - **5 – Very Evident (Palagiang Nakikita):** The teacher consistently demonstrates the indicator.
  - **3 – Sometimes Evident (Paminsan-minsang Nakikita):** The teacher occasionally demonstrates the indicator.
  - **1 – Not Evident (Hindi Nakikita):** The teacher rarely or never demonstrates the indicator.

### Completing the Evaluation:
1. Read each question carefully under each category (such as *Expertise of Content*, *Instructional Skillfulness*, *Classroom Management*, etc.).
2. Select your rating (from **1 to 5**) for each indicator by clicking the corresponding score circle.
3. Scroll to the bottom to the **Comments and Feedback** text area. You can write constructive suggestions, appreciation, or general remarks for the teacher.
4. Click the **Submit Evaluation** button.
5. A confirmation message will appear. Once submitted, your ratings are securely recorded, and the teacher card on your dashboard will update to **Evaluated**.

> **Note:** All student evaluations are completely confidential. Teachers will only see aggregated scores and anonymous feedback.

## 6. Viewing School Announcements

To stay updated with announcements from the school administration:

1. Click **Announcements** in the top navigation bar.
2. The **Announcement Board** displays recent announcements with:
   - Announcement title, publication date, and time.
   - Category tags (such as *General*, *Evaluation*, *Event*, *Urgent*, or *Academic*).
   - **Pinned Notices:** Important or urgent announcements are pinned at the very top with a pin icon.
3. You can filter announcements by clicking the category tabs at the top.
4. Unread announcements will have a subtle indicator. You can click **Mark as read** on individual posts or click **Mark all as read** at the top.

## 7. Managing Your Profile and Changing Your Password

To view your personal details or update your password:

1. Click your name or avatar in the top right corner and select **Settings** (or click **Settings** in the navigation bar).
2. The Settings page contains two sections:

### Profile Information Card (Left Side)
- Displays your **Full Name**, **Student ID Number**, and **Email Address**.
- Displays your **Grade Level**, **Section / Block**, and **School Level** (e.g., *Junior High School* or *Elementary*).

### Security & Password Card (Right Side)
To change your password:
1. Enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter your **New Password** (minimum 8 characters, must include numbers and special symbols).
3. Re-enter the new password in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.
5. A success notification will confirm the change.

---

## 8. Signing Out

When you have finished using the application, especially on a shared or school computer:

1. Click your profile avatar or name in the top right corner.
2. Click **Sign Out**.
3. You will be logged out safely and returned to the sign-in page.
