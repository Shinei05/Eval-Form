## 1. Signing In to the Teacher Portal

To access your faculty account:

1. Open **EduRate** in your web browser.
2. On the welcome page, click the **Teacher** role card (identified by an open book icon).
3. In the sign-in modal window:
   - Enter your registered **Teacher Email Address**.
   - Enter your **Password**.
4. Click the **Sign In** button.
5. If your credentials are correct and the evaluation schedule is open, you will be taken to your **Teacher Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Password Reset and Account Recovery

If you ever forget your password:

1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the Teacher Sign-In window.
2. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Verification Code**.
3. A 6-digit numeric security code will be sent to your email inbox.
4. Enter the code in the verification field and click **Verify Code**.
5. Create a new strong password in the **New Password** field and confirm it in **Confirm Password**.
   - *Requirement:* Must be at least 8 characters long and contain a combination of numbers and special symbols (e.g. `!`, `@`, `#`, `$`, `%`, etc.).
6. Click **Change Password** to complete the reset.

## 3. Evaluation Period Availability

Evaluations take place during specific scheduled evaluation periods set by the school administration (e.g., *1st Period*, *2nd Period*, *3rd Period*, or *4th Period*).

- **Active Period:** When a period is open, you can log in, view your colleague list, and submit peer evaluations.
- **Closed Period:** If no evaluation period is currently active, logging in will take you to the **"Evaluations Closed"** page informing you that the evaluation window is temporarily closed.

## 4. Navigating the Teacher Dashboard

Your dashboard is designed for straightforward peer review management.

### Top Banner & Period Information
- Displays your greeting and full name.
- Shows the current active **School Year** and **Evaluation Period** (e.g., *2025-2026 | 1st Period*).

### Security Warning Notice
- If your account is still using an initial or unverified default password, a yellow banner will prompt you to update your password in Settings.

### Summary Statistics
Three metric cards summarize your evaluation progress:
- **Colleagues to Evaluate:** Total number of faculty members available for peer review (your own name is automatically excluded from this list).
- **Evaluations Completed:** Number of peer evaluations you have already completed.
- **Pending Evaluations:** Number of colleagues awaiting your evaluation.

### Search and Filters
Easily locate specific faculty members using the filter bar:
- **Search Colleagues:** Type a teacher's first name, last name, or subject.
- **Grade Level Filter Dropdown:** Filter colleagues by grade level (*All Grades & Levels*, *Elementary (Grades 4–6)*, *Junior High (Grades 7–10)*, or specific grades *Grade 4* through *Grade 10*).
- **Subject Filter Dropdown:** View teachers belonging to a specific department or subject area (e.g., *Science*, *Mathematics*, *Filipino*, etc.).
- **Hide Evaluated Toggle:** Check this option to hide colleagues you have already reviewed and focus only on pending evaluations.
- **Sort By:** Sort the list alphabetically by *Name*, by *Subject*, or by *Quarter*.

### Colleague Cards
Each faculty colleague is displayed on an individual card featuring:
- Colleague's full name and email.
- Subject department.
- Grade level badge (e.g., *Elementary (Grades 4–6)*, *Junior High (Grades 7–10)*).
- Quarter and academic year.
- **Status Indicator:**
  - **Pending / Not Evaluated** (Yellow): Shows a blue **Evaluate** button to start the peer evaluation.
  - **Evaluated** (Green): Indicates you have already submitted your peer evaluation for this colleague during the current period. The button is disabled to avoid duplicate submissions.

## 5. How to Conduct a Peer Evaluation

1. Find the colleague you wish to evaluate on your dashboard and click the **Evaluate** button.
2. The **Peer Evaluation Tool for Teachers** will open.

### Form Structure:
- **Evaluator & Teacher Info:** Shows your name as the evaluator, the colleague being evaluated, the subject, and the current date.
- **Rating Scale Legend:**
  - **5 – Very Evident / Outstanding:** Consistent, exemplary demonstration of the indicator.
  - **3 – Sometimes Evident / Satisfactory:** Regular demonstration of the indicator.
  - **1 – Not Evident / Needs Improvement:** Infrequent or absent demonstration of the indicator.
  - *Values 4 and 2 can also be selected for intermediate performance levels.*

### Submitting the Review:
1. Review each indicator organized under professional domain headers (such as *Expertise of Content*, *Instructional Skillfulness*, *Classroom Management*, *Professionalism*, etc.).
2. Select your rating (from **1 to 5**) for every question.
3. In the **Professional Feedback & Comments** section at the bottom, write constructive, professional observations and recommendations.
4. Click **Submit Evaluation**.
5. Once submitted, your peer review is recorded and the colleague's card on your dashboard will update to **Evaluated**.

> **Note on Confidentiality:** All peer evaluations are treated with strict confidentiality. Summaries provided to administration and teachers display aggregated ratings.

## 6. Accessing the Announcement Board

To view official notices, memos, and scheduling announcements:

1. Click **Announcements** in the top navigation bar.
2. The **Announcement Board** displays school notices in chronological order.
3. Features of the announcement feed:
   - **Pinned Notices:** Urgent announcements are pinned at the top with a special highlight.
   - **Category Filters:** Filter notices by *General*, *Evaluation*, *Event*, *Urgent*, or *Academic*.
   - **Read Status:** Click **Mark as read** on individual posts or click **Mark all as read** at the top right to keep your feed organized.

---

## 7. Managing Profile and Updating Password

To review your account profile or change your password:

1. Click your name or avatar in the top right corner and choose **Settings** (or click **Settings** in the navigation bar).
2. The Settings page provides two cards:

### Profile Information Card (Left)
- Displays your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Role** (Teacher).
- Displays your **Assigned Subject Department**, **Quarter/Year**, and **School Level** (e.g., *Elementary* or *Junior High School*).

### Password & Security Card (Right)
To change your password:
1. Enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter your **New Password** (at least 8 characters with numbers and special symbols).
3. Re-enter the new password in **Confirm New Password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.
5. A confirmation message will appear confirming that your password has been securely updated.

---

## 8. Signing Out

To securely exit your session:

1. Click your profile avatar or name in the top right corner of the screen.
2. Select **Sign Out**.
3. You will be logged out and returned to the sign-in screen.
